When clients ask about the cost of a software architecture review, my honest answer is always, "It depends, but the investment almost always pays for itself many times over." It's not a fixed price, but a tailored service. Costs range from a few thousand dollars for a focused review to significantly more for complex enterprise systems. Regardless of scale, you gain clarity, risk mitigation, and a clear path forward for your software's future.

## Why Even Consider a Software Architecture Review?

Before discussing cost, understand *why* a review is essential. Even if your system is mostly working, an outside team can prevent future issues. Common scenarios include:

*   **Performance Bottlenecks:** Slow application, user complaints, unknown root cause.
*   **Scalability Concerns:** Anticipating growth but unsure if the architecture can handle increased load.
*   **High Maintenance Costs:** Slow development, frequent bugs, high effort for small changes.
*   **Security Vulnerabilities:** Need expert eye on design weaknesses for compliance or after a scare.
*   **Team Turnover/Knowledge Gaps:** Loss of institutional knowledge, requiring documentation and current state understanding.
*   **Migrating to New Technologies:** Validating strategy for cloud, microservices, or refactoring legacy systems.

A review helps you avoid costly mistakes, focusing on proactive problem-solving.

## What Drives the Cost of a Software Architecture Review?

Several factors directly influence the price. Understanding these helps you scope needs and budget:

## 1. Complexity and Scale of the System

This is the biggest factor. A small web app is simpler to review than a distributed enterprise system with multiple microservices, cloud platforms, and complex integrations. More modules, code, dependencies, and business logic increase complexity.

## 2. Depth of the Review

High-level risk assessment costs less than a forensic analysis including code audits, security protocols, and performance testing. Scope is tailored to your concerns.

## 3. Availability and Quality of Documentation

Good documentation (diagrams, design docs, API specs) saves money by allowing faster understanding. Reverse-engineering or extensive interviews add to effort and cost.

## 4. Team Size and Expertise Required

A single senior architect for small projects differs from a team of specialists (security, performance, cloud) for larger systems. More specialized expertise and person-hours increase cost. At KruskalCode, we ensure the right experience for your stack.

## 5. Deliverables and Follow-up

A basic report is cheaper than workshops, a detailed roadmap, or ongoing advisory services. Comprehensive deliverables increase cost.

## 6. Urgency

Rapid turnaround may require dedicated resources and overtime, impacting the price. Thorough analysis benefits from adequate time.

## Practical Cost Ranges and What They Include

Exact figures vary, but here are general industry price ranges and expectations:

## Focused "Health Check" (e.g., $5,000 - $15,000 USD)

*   **What it is:** Quick, high-level assessment of 1-2 critical areas (e.g., performance, security, cloud readiness).
*   **Timeframe:** 1-2 weeks.
*   **Process:** Initial interviews, high-level documentation review, brief system overview, limited code review.
*   **Deliverables:** Concise report with key findings, major risks, high-level recommendations, often a presentation.
*   **Best for:** Smaller apps, early-stage startups, or specific urgent problems.

## Standard Architecture Review (e.g., $15,000 - $40,000 USD)

*   **What it is:** Comprehensive review of medium-sized apps or significant modules, covering scalability, maintainability, and initial security.
*   **Timeframe:** 3-6 weeks.
*   **Process:** In-depth interviews with stakeholders, detailed review of diagrams, key code, and infrastructure.
*   **Deliverables:** Detailed report with specific findings, categorized risks, actionable recommendations, high-level roadmap, and workshops.
*   **Best for:** Established small-to-medium businesses or critical parts of larger enterprise systems needing a solid architectural foundation.

## Enterprise-Level or Deep Dive Review (e.g., $40,000+ USD)

*   **What it is:** Full-scale, in-depth analysis of complex enterprise, distributed, or mission-critical systems. Often includes specialized security, performance, or cloud migration reviews.
*   **Timeframe:** 6-12+ weeks.
*   **Process:** Extensive engagement, comprehensive documentation review, detailed code audits, performance profiling, security assessments, infrastructure analysis. May use specific tools.
*   **Deliverables:** Comprehensive assessment report, detailed risk register, prioritized recommendations with estimated effort, strategic roadmap, multiple workshops, technical debt assessment, and technology recommendations.
*   **Best for:** Large enterprises, highly regulated industries, or complex systems undergoing significant transformation.

## When Is the Right Time for a Review?

While always beneficial, some moments are particularly opportune:

*   **Before a Major Investment:** Validate approach for large feature rollouts, refactors, or cloud migrations to prevent costly missteps.
*   **After Significant Growth:** Ensure architecture can sustain exploded user base or data volume.
*   **When Performance or Stability Issues Arise:** Proactive diagnosis saves time and money, preventing critical failures.
*   **During Team Transitions:** Help onboard new architects and solidify institutional knowledge when leadership changes.
*   **Periodically (Every 1-3 Years):** Regular check-ups keep stable systems aligned with evolving business needs and tech trends.

## FAQ

## What is a software architecture review?

A software architecture review is a systematic, independent assessment of a software system's design and structure. Its purpose is to identify potential risks, inefficiencies, and areas for improvement related to performance, scalability, security, maintainability, and overall alignment with business goals.

## How long does a typical architecture review take?

The duration varies significantly based on the system's complexity and the depth of the review. A focused health check might take 1-2 weeks, while a comprehensive enterprise-level review could span 6-12 weeks or more. We'll provide a detailed timeline after understanding your specific needs.

## What kind of information do you need from us for a review?

We typically request access to existing documentation (architecture diagrams, design documents, API specs), code repositories, infrastructure configurations, and performance metrics. We also conduct interviews with key stakeholders like developers, product managers, and operations teams.

## Can you review a system with limited or outdated documentation?

Yes, we can, but it will likely increase the time and cost of the review. When documentation is sparse, our team needs to spend more time reverse-engineering the system and interviewing personnel to understand its current state. We always recommend providing as much current information as possible.

## What are the main benefits of getting an architecture review?

Key benefits include identifying and mitigating risks (security, performance, scalability), improving system maintainability, reducing technical debt, optimizing development costs, validating strategic technology decisions, and gaining a clear roadmap for future improvements. It helps ensure your software can meet current and future business demands.

## Do you offer ongoing support after the review?

Yes, we can provide optional ongoing advisory services or follow-up consultations to support your team in implementing the recommendations from the review. This ensures the insights gained translate into tangible improvements over time.
